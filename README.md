## About Me

I'm a master's student at **Beijing University of Chemical Technology (BUCT)**, working with the **NanoChem Research Group** at the **State Key Laboratory of Chemical Resource Engineering**.

My main research focus is **AI for Science (AI4S)**, particularly **video models**, **computer vision**, and **deep learning**. I am interested in extracting useful information from scientific images and videos, and in making modern AI methods practical for real research workflows.

Alongside research, I build AI-powered tools and end-to-end software systems—from model experimentation and dataset preparation to APIs, applications, and deployment.

## Research Interests

- **AI4S & Scientific Video Understanding** — learning from visual and temporal observations of scientific processes.
- **Computer Vision** — segmentation, visual representation learning, and scientific image analysis.
- **Generative Models & Efficient Inference** — diffusion models and practical model acceleration.
- **AI Systems & Software Engineering** — engineering useful, maintainable tools around AI models.

## Selected Research & Projects

### 📄 Research: Efficient Diffusion Transformers

**[Rethinking What to Cache in Few-Step Diffusion Transformers: Solver-Aware Target Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03577)** · *arXiv, 2026*

Research on **AutoTarget**, a solver-aware approach to selecting cached tensors for efficient few-step image and video diffusion transformers. The work studies the relationship between cache reuse, sampling dynamics, and generation quality.

[Paper (PDF)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.03577) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03577)

### ⚡ PCBTool.AI — AI-Powered Circuit Engineering

An AI-assisted circuit engineering workflow featured in the **NVIDIA Developer Community**, covering requirements interpretation, component selection, bill-of-materials generation, and circuit-related code generation.

[Project Showcase](https://developer.nvidia.cn/community/projects/pcbtool-circuit-generator) · [Repository](https://github.com/JackWPP/PCBTool)
