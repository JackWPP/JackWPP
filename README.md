## About

I'm a master's student at **Beijing University of Chemical Technology (BUCT)**, working with the **NanoChem Research Group** at the **State Key Laboratory of Chemical Resource Engineering**.

My research focuses on **AI for Science (AI4S)**, **video models**, and **computer vision** — extracting useful information from scientific images and videos, and making modern AI methods practical for real research workflows.

Alongside research, I build AI-powered tools and end-to-end software systems, from model experimentation and dataset preparation to APIs, applications, and deployment.

> [!NOTE]
> **Now** — accelerating few-step diffusion transformers with solver-aware caching, and building better tooling for scientific video datasets.

## Selected Work

### 📄 AutoTarget — Efficient Few-Step Diffusion Transformers

**[Rethinking What to Cache in Few-Step Diffusion Transformers: Solver-Aware Target Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03577)**  
*arXiv, 2026*

A solver-aware approach to selecting cached tensors for efficient few-step image and video diffusion transformers. The work studies the relationship between cache reuse, sampling dynamics, and generation quality.

[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/Paper-PDF-B31B1B?style=flat-square&logo=adobeacrobatreader&logoColor=white)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.03577) [![arXiv](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2610.03577-B31B1B?style=flat-square&logo=arxiv&logoColor=white)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03577)

### ⚡ PCBTool.AI — AI-Powered Circuit Engineering

An AI-assisted circuit engineering workflow featured in the **NVIDIA Developer Community** — covering requirements interpretation, component selection, bill-of-materials generation, and circuit-related code generation.

[![NVIDIA Showcase](https://img.shields.io/badge/NVIDIA_Showcase-View-76B900?style=flat-square&logo=nvidia&logoColor=white)](https://developer.nvidia.cn/community/projects/pcbtool-circuit-generator) [![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-PCBTool-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/JackWPP/PCBTool)
